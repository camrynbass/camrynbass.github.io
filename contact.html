Gagné Lab — CRIUCPQ

The Gagné lab is located at the Centre de Recherche de l'Institut Universitaire de Cardiologie et de Pneumologie de Québec (CRIUCPQ) - a Université Laval research center.

Our multi-disciplinary team performs innovative pathological, molecular, and bioinformatic research on lung cancer, focusing on biomarker discovery and answering unmet clinical needs to improve patient outcome, experience, and prognostic accuracy.
